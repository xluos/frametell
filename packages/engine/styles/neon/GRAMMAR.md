# Neon Noir · grammar card

An original pack for the open-source Studio.

## In one sentence

Neon tubes flicker on with the voice, numbers glitch into place, a grid floor runs to the horizon.

## Picture

- **Ground and ink.** Paper #07060D, ink #FF2E88; the picture sits in a generous margin. A colour means one thing for the whole film: red = the lead accent (the answer, the main path); blue = the second accent (support and comparison).
- **Line.** Thin glowing lines on near-black, a node grid; no purple-blue gradient washes over the whole frame.
- Keep text short: an element at most 8 Chinese characters (about 4 English words); body text at least 36px, labels at least 28px. Numbers and names sit on the object they describe.

## Motion and camera

- Every change is drawn while it is spoken; nothing important appears all at once. One visible change every 2–4 seconds; the page settles for about a second before it turns.
- The camera holds wide by default and pushes briefly into the key change. Data (a packet, a traveller dot) actually moves along the paths with liveRough/follow/tokens.move; arrows alone are not enough.
- No idle looping, floating particles or bouncy decoration.

## Pacing

Good for: AI 与未来科技、创业与趋势、互联网文化. 10–20 seconds per page, 3–5 pages a minute. Open on the concrete situation, end on the changed state that answers the opening question.

## Never

- A title plus three cards with arrows (a slide deck); a page filled with small text; neon/glow where the style is paper-based.
- More than two accent colours on one screen; faces unless the grammar card above says the style draws characters.
- Photos, 3D, and effects from another style.
