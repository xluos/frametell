# Broadsheet · grammar card

This pack draws on: Chart grammar adapted from huashu-art-motion (MIT, © 2026 alchaincyf). Techniques adapted, not copied; follow that project's own licence for anything beyond these rules.

## In one sentence

The financial front page come alive: salmon newsprint, serif headlines, charts that land on the spoken word.

## Picture

- **Ground and ink.** Paper #FFF1E5, ink #990F3D; the picture sits in a generous margin. A colour means one thing for the whole film: red = the lead accent (the answer, the main path); blue = the second accent (support and comparison).
- **Line.** Newspaper columns, serif headlines, chart grammar: thin rules and one statistic red.
- Keep text short: an element at most 8 Chinese characters (about 4 English words); body text at least 36px, labels at least 28px. Numbers and names sit on the object they describe.

## Motion and camera

- Every change is drawn while it is spoken; nothing important appears all at once. One visible change every 2–4 seconds; the page settles for about a second before it turns.
- The camera holds wide by default and pushes briefly into the key change. 
- No idle looping, floating particles or bouncy decoration.

## Pacing

Good for: 财经、经济、市场与投资. 10–20 seconds per page, 3–5 pages a minute. Open on the concrete situation, end on the changed state that answers the opening question.

## Never

- A title plus three cards with arrows (a slide deck); a page filled with small text; neon/glow where the style is paper-based.
- More than two accent colours on one screen; faces unless the grammar card above says the style draws characters.
- Photos, 3D, and effects from another style.
