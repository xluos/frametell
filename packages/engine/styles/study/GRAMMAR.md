# Quiet study · grammar card

A thoughtful explanation on warm paper: ink drawings, restrained washes and notes beside the objects they explain. The runtime's default palette and paper background are this style's appearance.

## Picture

- Use warm paper, dark ink and one or two muted accents. Keep a stable meaning for each accent.
- Build around one large subject-specific drawing, annotated passage, map or worked example. Use uneven but balanced compositions rather than a repeated title and three cards.
- Put brief labels next to the thing they name. Body text at least 36px; small labels at least 28px. The bottom sixth is reserved for subtitles.
- Fine, slightly irregular ink outlines and pale colour washes. Leave paper visible; no heavy shadows or shiny panels.
- A literal example can carry the explanation. Add a metaphor only when it makes a relationship easier to understand.

## Motion and pacing

- Start with the main object already present; add marks, arrows and annotations when the voice explains them.
- Preserve earlier evidence while working through a cause or comparison. Let one meaningful change lead each moment.
- Use a mostly fixed camera with an occasional brief push to a detail and a return to the whole.
- Let worked examples take longer than transitions. Pause briefly on a completed result.
- Finish by resolving the opening question in the same drawing.

## Avoid

- Reading the page's labels as the whole narration; paragraphs of prose on screen.
- A repeated card grid, unrelated stock icons, decorative motion, neon or photographic textures.
